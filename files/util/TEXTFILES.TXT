                    THE TEXFILE AREA: AN INDEX

Everything in this area is plain text, written for this board. Read
them on the board with the [F]ile areas command, or grab the download
link if you are calling from a browser.

  MODEMS.TXT     What the speeds and the letters on the box mean, and
                 how to stop your line from dropping every night.

  ANSI.TXT       How a screen is drawn out of 256 characters. Read
                 this before you try to draw your own menu.

  DOORS.TXT      Door game etiquette, plus a short guide to every game
                 in the door menu on this board.

  SYSOP.TXT      What running a board actually costs, the rules you
                 need to write before you need them, and why the first
                 week should be two nodes and not four.

  TEXTFILES.TXT  This file. If you are reading it, the index works.

WHAT BELONGS HERE
  Documents, guides, lists, and anything typed by a person rather than
  produced by a program. No executables, no archives of somebody
  else's software, no phone codes.

  If you write something worth reading, upload it and leave a note for
  the sysop. Credit yourself; unsigned text helps nobody.

                          -- the sysop of the Gate
