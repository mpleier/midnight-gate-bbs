                     DOOR GAME ETIQUETTE
                  (and what is on this board)

A door game is a program the board hands you for a while. The board
takes the call, the game takes over the screen, and when you quit the
board picks the line back up. That is the whole trick.

THE RULES EVERYONE AGREES ON, MOSTLY
  1. Do not leave a game running to burn someone else's node. The
     sysop can see the node table and will see you.
  2. Turns reset daily. Waiting until 12:01 to attack someone who is
     asleep is legal and everyone will still hate you for it.
  3. Do not multi-account. The sysop can see your modem signature, the
     time you call, and the way you type. You are not as anonymous as
     you think.
  4. If you find a bug that gives you infinite gold, tell the sysop.
     Using it is how a good board dies.
  5. Do not beg for turns. Earn them: post messages, upload files,
     call in and be pleasant.
  6. Attack whoever you like in character. Keep it in character.
     "Your realm smells of wet wool" is fine. Anything about someone's
     family is not.

WHAT IS IN THE DOOR MENU HERE

                             dragon. Three fights a day and a lot of
                             patience.
  DRAGON'S KEEP              our house RPG. Same idea, meaner keep.
  TRADE WARS 3001            space trading, sixty sectors, real
                             danger in the lanes.
  THE PIT                    arena combat, blade against blade.
  BARREN REALMS              empire building. Farms, armies, grudges.
  DOMINIONS                  star systems, fog of war, delayed fleets.
  BLACKJACK                  cards. Real credits. Terrible decisions.
  FRESHWATER FISHING         nine spots, ten casts, no one shooting
                             at you. Recommended after a bad day.
  WASTELAND: OVERKILL        the wasteland, a scanner, and a timing
                             game that will get you killed.

TIPS FROM PEOPLE WHO HAVE LOST A LOT OF TURNS
  - In the dragon game, bank your gold before you go into the forest.
  - In the trading game, never fly with a full hold and no fuel.
  - In the arena, corners are death. Keep two exits.
  - In the empire game, farms before armies. Always.
  - In the fishing game, strike late, not early.

                          -- the sysop of the Gate
