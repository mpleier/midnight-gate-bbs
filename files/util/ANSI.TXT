                        ANSI ART: A PRIMER
                     for callers who want to draw

Every board you call is lying to you about its own age. The menus are
not photographs. They are characters: the same 256 symbols that came
with the IBM PC in 1981, arranged until they look like a picture.

THE CHARACTER SET
  Codepage 437 has 256 slots. The first 128 are plain ASCII. The top
  128 are where the art lives: box drawing lines, block fills, shading
  patterns, Greek letters, and a handful of card suits that nobody uses.

  The four you will use most:
      █  full block        full cell of colour
      ▀  upper half        top half of the cell
      ▄  lower half        bottom half
      ░▒▓  shading        25%, 50%, 75% dither

  Two half blocks stacked vertically give you a square. That is how a
  "pixel" is made in a text mode that has no pixels.

COLOUR
  Sixteen colours, eight of them bright. Each character can have a
  foreground and a background, which means you can fake a solid area
  by setting a background colour and printing spaces. Art packs are
  mostly background colour and blocks.

  A gradient is just a ramp: pick four colours, walk across the row,
  step to the next one every few characters. Nobody can tell the
  difference between a careful ramp and a clever one on a VGA monitor.

DRAWING SOMETHING THAT LOOKS DELIBERATE
  1. Draw the frame first, in double lines, and keep it straight. An
     off-by-one border ruins an otherwise good screen.
  2. Leave a margin. Art that touches the edges looks like a mistake.
  3. Shade in one direction only. Mixed light sources look like noise.
  4. Highlights on top and left, shadows on bottom and right. Every
     logo you have ever admired does exactly this.
  5. Type is not art. A logo needs weight: fill the letters, then add
     a one-column extrusion to the right to give it depth.

TOOLS
  The ANSI editor on this board is your business, but the old hands
  still use TheDraw and call it done. Whatever you use, save as plain
  bytes, not as a picture. If it opens in a word processor it is not
  ANSI.

SENDING ART HERE
  Upload it in the file area, or leave the sysop a note. Credit
  yourself. Half the art in this scene is unsigned and the artists
  deserve better.
